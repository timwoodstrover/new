one day there will be a gun and it will just shoot nothing but water. they will call it the water gun. it will be full of water and they will use it to refill the sea.

although the preacher spoke with command, many were suspect. for the sea is large and full of salt, and the physical viability of a gun that could fit enough water in the chamber, and then enough salt on top of that while still making space for staples and the odd fish or shrimp, became dubious to them with protracted thought.
